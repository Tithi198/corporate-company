Fairway Shipping Lines Ltd.

Fairway Shipping Lines Ltd. is a sister concern of Phoenix Group, engaged in international import and export activities. Our core business is sourcing essential food commodities, agricultural products, and construction materials from global markets and supplying them throughout the local market in Bangladesh.

We are import food and agricultural commodities from the various countries of the world covering China, India, Russia, and other international markets too. Our key imported products include wheat, mustard seeds, maize, soybean meal, pulses, salt, sugar, and other essential food items. These products play an important role in meeting the growing requirements of Bangladesh's food, agricultural, and commercial sectors.

Our goal is to ensure a trusted supply of essential commodities by maintaining professional relationships with international suppliers and local business partners. We are closely monitoring product availability, market demand, quality requirements, and commercial conditions when selecting products and sourcing markets.

Fairway Shipping Lines Ltd. has been importing since 2022 a wide range of construction materials. Our construction material portfolio includes stone, clinker, slag, fly ash, gypsum, and other related products. These materials are widely used in cement production, building construction, infrastructure development, and various industrial applications.

The expansion into construction materials reflects our commitment to business growth and diversification. As Bangladesh's construction and infrastructure sectors continue to develop, we aim to contribute by supporting the availability of essential construction raw materials from international sources.

Fairway Shipping Lines Ltd., we understand that international trade requires careful planning, clear communication, proper documentation, and effective coordination. Our business approach is based on professionalism, transparency, reliability, and long-term cooperation. We strive to understand market requirements and ensure smooth coordination between overseas suppliers and local customers.

As part of Phoenix Group, we are committed to building strong and lasting relationships with our suppliers, customers, and business associates. Our mission is to source essential products from global markets and supply them to the local market through responsible, efficient, and well-coordinated trading activities.

Looking ahead, Fairway Shipping Lines Ltd. aims to become a trusted name in Bangladesh's import and export sector. We will continue to expand our product portfolio, strengthen our international sourcing network, explore new business opportunities, and contribute to the country's food supply, industrial development, and infrastructure growth.
